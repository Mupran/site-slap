A slap game that wrecks your website.

Two stick figures, one ring, one script tag. Hold to charge a slap. Hit hard enough and the loser leaves the ring, lands somewhere on your page, and breaks it. This page is the demo, so everything on it is fair game.

I built this with Claude. It is open source under the MIT licence, so anyone can add it to their own website and wreck it.

Go on. Break something.

The longer you hold, the further they go

  1. A patA quick tap. Mostly an insult.
  2. The ropesThey stagger back and the ropes fling them home.
  3. Out of the ringThey land in a nearby section and scatter what is there.
  4. Across the pageA long flight to somewhere far away, with the page scrolling to follow.
  5. Into the headerThe navigation tilts and its links fall off.
  6. Through the websiteA full charge punches a hole straight through the page.

What you get

One file

No dependencies and no build step. It draws on canvas and keeps its interface in a shadow root, so your styles and its styles stay apart.

Real damage, fully reversible

It moves your actual elements with CSS transforms. One button puts every one of them back exactly as it was.

Two players or one

Share a screen and take turns, on buttons or the A and L keys. Or switch to play the computer.

Install

Paste this before the closing </body> tag. A “Slap this site” button appears in the corner.

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Mupran/site-slap@1.0.0/site-slap.js"></script>

To place the ring inside your page, as it is above, point it at an element.

<div id="ring"></div>
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Mupran/site-slap@1.0.0/site-slap.js"
        data-target="#ring"></script>

Options

data-targetCSS selector of an element to hold the ring. Without it, a floating launcher is added.
data-computerStart against the computer instead of a second player.
data-positionleft or right, the corner for the floating launcher.
data-labelText for the floating launcher button.
data-manualLoad without setting anything up, then call SiteSlap.mount() yourself.
